Privacy Policy

Almanac · Last updated 22 August 2026

Almanac has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. It does not track you, does not show ads, and contains no analytics or advertising software. Almost everything it knows stays on your device.

This policy explains the little that does leave your device, and why.

What stays on your device

Almanac keeps a small private database inside the app’s own storage. Nothing in it is uploaded anywhere. It holds:

  • A random identifier created the first time you open the app. It is used only to pick which passage you see, so that two people on the same day in the same weather do not always get the same one. It is not tied to you, your device, or any account, and it is never sent anywhere.
  • The passages you have read, with the date, the time of day, and the approximate location they were chosen for.
  • A short-lived copy of the most recent weather, so the app does not ask for it again every time you open it.
  • Which passage collections you own.

The passages themselves ship inside the app. Reading them requires no network at all.

What leaves your device

Sent to What Why
MET Norway
api.met.no		 An approximate location — coordinates rounded to about 1.1 km To look up the current weather, which decides the passage
RevenueCat, and Apple or Google Purchase and receipt information, tied to an anonymous identifier Only if you buy a collection — to confirm the purchase and restore it later

Your exact location is never sent. Coordinates are deliberately rounded before any request, because the weather does not change over a city block and the app has no reason to know where you are more precisely than that.

MET Norway is the Norwegian Meteorological Institute. Weather data is used under CC BY 4.0. See their privacy information.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play; Almanac never sees your payment details. Purchase state is managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier it generates itself — not your name, email, or Apple or Google account.

Location is optional

You can decline the location permission and use Almanac fully. If you do, choose a city in the app instead and the weather is looked up for that city. You can change or revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

What Almanac does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, or crash reporting.
  • No profiles, no behavioural targeting.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No accounts, and no email address is ever asked for.

The app stores each platform provides may collect their own diagnostics as part of app distribution and payment. That is governed by Apple’s and Google’s policies, not this one.

Children

Almanac is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Almanac stores is on your device, so deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server copy and no account to close. Purchases are held by the App Store or Google Play and can be restored from the app if you reinstall.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
